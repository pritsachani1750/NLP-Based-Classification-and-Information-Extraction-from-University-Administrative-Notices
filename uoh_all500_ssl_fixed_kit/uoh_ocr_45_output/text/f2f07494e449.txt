Annexure - I:

Instructions / Terms and Conditions
1. The sealed quotations are invited for supply of Lab Chemicals, consumables, glassware, minor
equipment etc. of reputed brands which are scheduled in the Annexure-II from reputed
manufacturers or their authorized dealers with all required documents and should be placed in
one single cover and the same should be addressed to The Assistant Registrar, Central
Procurement Unit, Room No. 122, University of Hyderabad, Gachibowli, Hyderabad -
500046.

2. The bidders should furnish bid security (EMD) of Rs. 1,00,000/- (Rupees One lakh
only) along with their bids. The bid security/EMD is to be obtained in favour of Finance
Officer, University of Hyderabad, Hyderabad-46 in the form of Account Payee Demand Draft,
Fixed Deposit Receipt, Banker's Cheque, or Bank Guarantee from any of the Nationalized,
Scheduled and Commercial Bank. The bid security shall remain valid for a period of 45 days
beyond final bid validity period.

3. The bidders shall quote only the maximum percentage of discount offered on MRP invariably
and strictly against the brands mentioned in Annexure-II, otherwise quotation will not be
considered. The discount so offered shall be automatically applicable to all items/products of
that brand. Make/brand quoted should not be different from that mentioned in Annexure-II.
The percentage of discount quoted on MRP shall hold good for the entire duration of the
contract.

4. The bidder quoting the highest percentage of discount on MRP, subject to fulfilling other terms
and conditions will be declared as successful bidder. The H1 bidder identified for each brand
will have to supply chemicals, consumables, glassware & equipment etc. of that brand item at
his offered discount percentage on MRP. The University Departments/Schools may place
individual purchase orders for the items belonging to the specific brands to the H1 bidder who
shall supply the same against the respective purchase orders within 7 to 10 working days.

5. After satisfactory supply and acceptance of goods the vendor shall raise individual invoices
against the respective purchase orders in triplicate and submit to the issuing authority of
purchase order within 7 days of completion of delivery. @

6. Bidders must submit OEM/authorization letter along with their bids from the competent/brand
authorities for the brands quoted.

7. Companies whose beneficial ownership is in a country which shares land border with India must
first register with DPIIT and proof of such registration must be submitted along with the bid.

8. Figures should not be over written. Over written figured should be scored out and rewritten by
duly initialing the same by the Competent Authority.

9. Details of discounts, schemes or special offers or special rates for government institutions, if
any, should be mentioned clearly.

10. When a Rate Enquiry runs into several pages, each page must be signed by the bidders.
Against those items for which a bidder cannot quote, please mark "NOT QUOTING". Unsigned
quotations will not be considered.
