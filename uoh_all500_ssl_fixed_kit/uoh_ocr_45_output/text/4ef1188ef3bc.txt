TENDER-NOTICE

Sealed tenders are invited from reputed and experienced catering service providers for
running the Canteen Facility at East Campus, University of Hyderabad, following the
termination of the previous contract due to non-functioning and unsatisfactory services.
1. Scope of Work:
Operation and management of the canteen for students, faculty, and staff.
Provision of hygienic, nutritious, and reasonably priced food and beverages.

Maintenance of cleanliness, hygiene, and proper record-keeping.
Compliance with all University and statutory regulations.

2. Eligibility Criteria:

Registered catering service provider with valid trade license.

Minimum 3 years of experience in catering services at educational or institutional establishments.
Proven track record of quality service, hygiene, and customer satisfaction.

Ability to comply with the University’s rules and regulations.
