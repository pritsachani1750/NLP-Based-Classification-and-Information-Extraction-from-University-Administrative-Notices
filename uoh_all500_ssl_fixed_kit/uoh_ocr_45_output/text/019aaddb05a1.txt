ANNEXURE -— I
Introduction:

The University of Hyderabad is a premier academic and research institution in India, recently
conferred with status of Institute of Eminence. The campus has nearly 22 students’ hostels, 13
schools, 41 departments/Centres and laboratories. The administration building is its nodal point. The
University serves above 5000 students with 440 faculties and 300 quarters and nearly 10 big/small
canteens apart from Shopping Complex, guest houses and auditoria and a road network of 30 KMs.

This being the topography of the University of Hyderabad, the University aspires to have an
eco-friendly garbage disposal system / zero waste management system at Campus and seeks a
professional firm with experience to dispose garbage generated around all these areas, besides
handling all types of waste material from all its hostels, labs, departments, quarters, canteens and
guest house etc., Further, there are thousands of trees etc which seasonally generates abundant dry
leaves. All these dry leaves need to be collected and recycled. Apart from attending to all these works,
bio-waste also has to be processed so as to make useful energy or vermi-compost etc.
