The Vice-Chancellor extended a warm welcome to the members and informed
the Council that the XXV Convocation of the University would be held at 11.00
a.m. on October 7, 2025, at the Brahma Kumari’s Global Peace Auditorium,
Gachibowli.

The Council was further informed that Dr. Krishna Ella, Founder & Executive
Chairman, Bharat Biotech International Limited would be the Chief Guest and
will deliver the Convocation address, while Shri Jishnu Dev Varma, Hon’ble
Governor of Telangana and Chief Rector of the University agreed to attend the
Convocation as a Guest of Honour and Justice L. Narasimha Reddy, Chancellor
of the University would preside over the event.

Item No. Spl. EC:2025:1

To consider the award of degrees to the successful graduates and award of
medals at the XXV Convocation on October 7, 2025

The Council noted that 1,717 degrees are being awarded to successful
graduates in various streams and 182 medals (Donor Medals, University
Medals, SC/ST Medals, OBC Medals etc.) would be presented to the meritorious
students at the Convocation for the year 2025.

The Council has resolved to approve the award of degrees to successful
graduates and the award of medals to meritorious students.

Chancellor’s Awards:

The Council approved the recommendations of the Committee constituted for
the Chancellor’s awards on the following faculty of the University to be
conferred at the XXV Convocation of the University in recognition of their
contribution in terms of both teaching and research in the University.
