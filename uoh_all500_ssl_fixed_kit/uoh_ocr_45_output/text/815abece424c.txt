Item No.AC:95:2025:04: Report on admissions to the Ph.D. Programmes for the Academic
Year 2024-25.

The report presented by the Controller of Examinations on admission to the Ph.D. programmes for the
Academic Year 2024-25 was noted by the Council. The Council noted that an admission notification
for all the Ph.D. programmes for Academic Year 2025-26 has been released by the Office of the
Controller of Examinations and that the 22 academic units have opted to offer admission based on the
Entrance Exam to be held by the University and 20 academic units have decided to grant admissions
based on the UGC NET/ CSIR NET scores.

Chairman informed the House that the admissions to the Ph.D. programmes for last academic year
was held in two different modes, that is, by conducting the entrance exam and based on the scores of
UGC NET/CSIR NET as per the options exercised by the academic unit concerned. The admissions
to these programmes were concluded by conducting admission counselling and operation of wait list
and the process of admissions was formally closed in the month of February, 2025. At the end 109
seats in total remained unfilled. Out of which 17 seats for the streams for which entrance exams were
held and 92 seats against the NET based admission mode. It was also informed to the House that about
fifty percent of vacant seats were meant for reserved categories. The chief reason for said percentage
of reserved seats being vacant was on account of the fact that the candidates belonging to these
categories were admitted against the un-reserved category and hence there were no takers the seats

meant for them.

Page 7 of 16

===== PAGE 8 (OCR) =====
Keeping the above experience in view, the Chairman requested all the academic units to pay serious
attention towards the above phenomenon and to choose the admission mode carefully which can draw
a healthy competition and help the University fill all the seats. Accordingly, the Chairman advised all
the unit Heads to rework on it and to communicate their decision to the Controller of Examinations to
effect necessary changes in the admission notification for 2025-26 by bringing suitable amendments.
The House agreed to allow time for the academic units to act upon the above suggestion of the
Chairman and to communicate their decision to the CE latest by 2 May 2025.

The Council noted that the results of UGC-CSIR NET exam held in the month of January 2025 are
yet to be out and thus the candidates aspiring for admission into the University of Hyderabad will be
at disadvantage if the last date for submission of applications is not extended beyond 5 May 2025 for
the programmes for which NET scores are considered. Accordingly, it was resolved to extend the last
date for submission of application for NET based admissions up to 30 May 2025 and to include ICMR
JRF to be eligible for Ph.D. programmes in School of Life Sciences.

The Council noted that as per the guidelines issued by the UGC during March 2024, the candidates
awarded JRF shall be selected based on the performance in the interview out of 30 marks. Whereas,
the selection of category 2 & 3 of NET qualified candidates are based on combined marks scored by
them in the NET and the interview. The members felt that this situation is likely to put the Non-JRF
candidates to the disadvantage. Hence, the Council resolved that an Admission Committee may be
constituted to find out possible ways and means of appropriating the seats to the Non-JRF (category
2 & 3 of NET scores) candidates also, in accordance with UGC guidelines.

Item No.AC:95:2025:12: Payment of Scholarship under Direct Beneficiary Transfer (DBT)
Mode by the Social Welfare Department of State of Telangana to SC category students from the
Academic Year 2024-25 onwards.

The Controller of Examinations and the Chairman of the Council informed to the House that the
Government of Telangana has decided to remit the social welfare scholarship amounts directly into
the accounts of the beneficiaries under Direct Beneficiary Transfer (DBT) Mode by the respective
Social Welfare Departments through National Scholarship Portal from the academic year 2023-24.
Accordingly, the students belonging to the Scheduled Caste of State of Telangana and are eligible for
sanction of social welfare scholarship will be receiving the tuition fee, mess charges etc directly into
their bank accounts. As per the rules of the University such students will not be considered as social
welfare scholarship holders and are not entitled for concession in payment of semester fee and
deferment of payment of mess charges. Hence, all the SC students irrespective their domicile shall pay
full amount of fee from the academic year 2025-26 and the students who were granted concession
during the 2023-24 and 2024-25 shall clear their das by paying full amount of fee.
