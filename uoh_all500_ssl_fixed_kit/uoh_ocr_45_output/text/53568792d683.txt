APPEAL TO THE STUDENTS’ ORGANISATIONS

Please refer to the appeals dated January 18, 2023, and October 5, 2023, made by the undersigned
to all student organisations regarding the issue of defacing the walls of university campus buildings

through paintings, pasting notices, etc.

It is reminded that all student organisations should adhere to the directions issued by the Registrar’s
Office in Circular No. UH/REG/S-10/2022 dated October 4, 2022. This circular clearly states that
“defacing of the walls of the University in any manner is prohibited. Posters and bills should
only be posted on the boards provided for that purpose in designated areas.” Any violation of

these guidelines by any student organisation would disrupt our campus's peaceful ecosystem.

Despite these appeals, it has been reported by the Security Department of the University that some
student organisations are still defacing the walls of campus buildings with paintings and other
materials, detracting from the original appearance of the walls. As you are all aware, it is a tedious

and time-consuming task for the university to remove such unauthorised defacement.

I encourage all student organisations to adhere to the guidelines specified in the circular. It is

important that we work together to ensure the smooth operation of the University and promote a
