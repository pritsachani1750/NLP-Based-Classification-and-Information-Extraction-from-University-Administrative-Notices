incorporated in the Minutes appropriately.

FC:88:2025:4 | To consider and approve the draft Annual Accounts of the University for the Financial

submission to C&AG of India for conduct of Audit

The Finance Officer presented to the Committee the highlights in the financial statements
(viz, Receipts & Payments, Income & Expenditure and Balance Sheet) from the draft
Annual Accounts for the FY 2024-25 and provided clarification to the observations made by
the JS&FA, MoE in their comments on the Agenda.

The Committee then considered and approved the draft Annual Accounts for the financial
year 2024-25 and recommended for placing the same before the Executive Council for its
approval and subsequent submission to the C&AG for conducting the audit. Further, the
Committee authorized the Vice-Chancellor to make corrections, if any, in the draft Annual
Accounts, wherever considered necessary before submission to Audit.

FC:88:2025:7_| Physical and Financial Progress of the ongoing construction activities

The University Engineer made a Power Point Presentation on the progress of all the
construction works. The Committee noted the same and advised the University to

e — ensure that Works / Building Committee of UoH monitor and approve all the ongoing construction
works periodically to avoid cost and time overruns.

e — ensure that abstract cost and rate conformity certificate and other codal formalities for each building
project are as per the relevant UGC guidelines / relevant provisions of GFR 2017 and CVC.

e ensure that award of civil works are strictly as per the relevant provisions of GFRs and related CVC
guidelines.

FC:88:2025:9 | Status of pending Audit Paras
Noted. The Committee advised the University to

e ensure that the remaining pending Audit Paras are dropped at the earliest by providing required
information / clarification to the AG Audit.

e comply with the suggestions / instructions of the Audit, if any, to settle/drop the audit paras on
priority.

e — furnish remedial/corrective ATNs to the Audit timely and lapses, if any, in furnishing the same may
be viewed seriously.

e hold regular meetings of the Standing Audit Committee to review all pending Audit Paras and settle
the same

remaining paras also as soon as possible.
