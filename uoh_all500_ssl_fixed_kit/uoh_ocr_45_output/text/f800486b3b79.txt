APPEAL TO THE STUDENTS

Sub: Prohibition of alcohol and narcotics consumption on the campus — Reg.

aR

I want to address a serious issue that has come to my attention: the consumption of alcohol and narcotics
in the hostels and various areas of our campus, particularly during the night. This behavior disrupts our
campus environment and, in some tragic instances, has led to the loss of student lives.

A recent incident at Mens’ Hostel-I highlights this concern, where a student fell from the third floor and
sustained severe injuries to his kidneys, spleen, and bones, along with significant brain injuries, ultimately
resulting in his untimely death.

We are deeply saddened by the loss of this young student, who had a promising future ahead of him. Such
a tragedy impacts not only his family but also our entire campus community. I urge you to always consider
your loved parents and siblings when engaging in any activities related to substance abuse.

I would like to remind everyone that the consumption or possession of drugs, illicit alcohol, and other
prohibited substances is strictly prohibited on our campus. This is in accordance with the Narcotic Drugs
and Psychotropic Substances Act of 1985 (NDPS Act), enacted by the Government of India.

I urge all students to take this matter seriously and avoid any direct or indirect involvement with drug use
or the encouragement of such behavior. Your cooperation is essential in maintaining a drug-free campus
and fostering a safe and peaceful environment for everyone.

Students play a vital role in keeping our campus drug-free. If you notice any drug-related incidents on
campus, please report them immediately. You can do this by calling the anti-drug helpline at 040-6679 1005
or 040-23131005, or by contacting Ext. 1005. You can also email us at <antidruguoh@uohyd.ac.in>.

Let us commit to abstaining from alcohol and drugs on campus and focus on our studies, as each of you has
great potential for a bright future.

Thank you for your attention to this important matter.
