FC:90:2026:3

To consider and approve the draft Annual Accounts of the University for the Financial
Year 2025-26 for placing before the Executive Council for approval and onward
submission to C&AG of India for conduct of Audit

The Finance Officer presented to the Committee the highlights in the financial statements (viz.,
Receipts & Payments, Income & Expenditure and Balance Sheet) from the draft Annual
Accounts for the FY 2025-26 and provided required clarification on the points raised by the
JS&FA, IFD, MoE in their comments dt. 19.6.2026.

The Committee then considered and approved the draft Annual Accounts for the financial year
2025-26 and recommended for placing the same before the Executive Council for its approval
and subsequent submission to the C&AG for conducting the statutory audit. Further, the
Committee authorized the Vice-Chancellor to make corrections, if any, in the draft Annual
Accounts, wherever considered necessary before submission to Audit

FC:90:2026:4

Status of grants received in respect of Recurring (OH-31), Capital Assets (OH-35) and
Salaries (OH-36) vis-a-vis expenditure incurred thereof during the FY 2025-26 & FY 2026-
27 (till 31 May 2026)

The Committee noted the status of grants received under OH-31 — Recurring, OH-35 — Capital
Assets and OH-36 — Salary during the FY 2025-26 and FY 2026-27 till 31 May, 2026.

The Finance Officer has requested the UGC/MoE to

e consider, approve and release the negative opening balance of Rs. 855.58 lakhs under
OH-31-Recurring (Non-salary head) at the RE stage (2026-27).

e release funds as per the projections made by UoH under OH-31, OH-35 & OH-36.

e release funds under OH-35-Capital Assets for the FY 2026-27, at the earliest.

Vice-Chancellor & the Chairperson has emphasized the need to enhance the non-salary grant
under OH-31 due to the following reasons:

a) though the costs of maintenance of buildings, equipment, etc. are increasing each year, the
grant being sanctioned by the UGC/MoE is not being increased in that proportion.

b) the University’s campus is spread over 2300 acres and has more than 5000 students and
900 faculty + staff. As major portion of the University is still forest like area, the University
is forced to deploy more security guards for the safety of students / staff and incur a huge
amount on the security.

c) the University is operating about 23 hostels for the convenience of the students, which
require supporting staff.

d) the new buildings, class rooms, auditoriums constructed under HEFA & EWS are requiring
regular maintenance.

e) the cost of AMCs for several high value equipment is increasing year-on-year.

The Vice-Chancellor & Chairperson requested the UGC/MoE members present in the meeting
to consider the projections of UoH at RE stage and release adequate funds under OH-31-
Recurring (Non-salary head).
