Item No. EC:196:2026:13

To consider the implications of the UGC clarification dated 12-03-2024
regarding the counting of post-doctoral experience, including recognized
national and international fellowships, for direct recruitment and CAS
promotions of teachers.

The Council reviewed the implications of the UGC clarification dated
12.03.2024 on the inadmissibility of reckoning of post-doctoral experience,
including service rendered under various national and international
fellowships, as equivalent to that of an Assistant Professor in terms of Clause
10 of the UGC Regulations, 2010 & 2018, for direct recruitment and Career
Advancement Scheme (CAS) promotions of teachers.

The Council resolved that no further clarification is required, as the wording
of the UGC communication is clear and does not permit multiple
interpretations. The Council observed that the practice previously followed by
the University in this matter was not in alignment with the Commission’s
clarification. Accordingly, rather than seeking further clarifications, the
Council resolved to strictly adhere to the UGC’s clarification, effective from all
future faculty recruitment notifications.

To consider the cases of Shri P. Krishna, Laboratory Attendant and
existing employees in the Security cadre, deprived of the career
advancement prospects in the Cadre Recruitment Rules (CRR), 2023.

The Council acknowledged that the implementation of the Cadre Recruitment
Rules, 2023 (CRR-2023), by adopting the Model Cadre Recruitment Rules,
2023 (MCRR-2023) notified by the UGC, has inadvertently curtailed the
promotional prospects of existing employees of the University in the positions
of Laboratory Attendant, Assistant Security Officer and Security Officer,
thereby operating to their disadvantage and adversely affecting their career
advancement prospects.

Accordingly, the Council resolved that the CRR-2023 be reviewed with a view
to restoring promotional avenues for the above affected employees,
safeguarding their legitimate career advancement and ensuring fairness in
service conditions.

Since the said review and Visitor’s approval is a long-drawn process, the
Council resolved to explore the possibilities of affording the above affected
employees an opportunity to get promoted based on the norms existing prior
to notification of the CRR-2023.
