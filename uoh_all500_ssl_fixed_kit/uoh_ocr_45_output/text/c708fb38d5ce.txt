Technical Specifications

HiPIMS Power supply capable of handling reactive sputter process.

The proposed Bipolar High-Power Impulse Magnetron Sputtering (HiPIMS) Power Supply System
shall be a state-of-the-art, digitally controlled pulsed-plasma generation unit designed for advanced thin-
film deposition. The system must ensure high stability, reproducibility, and precision in both metallic
and reactive sputtering environments, in compliance with applicable international electrical and safety
standards. All subsystems shall provide full-field, real-time process monitoring to ensure accurate control
of plasma characteristics, peak current stability, and power delivery consistency during deposition.

[The HiPIMS power supply must be fully compatible with the existing sputtering system, including
magnetron sources, vacuum chamber interlocks, cooling provisions.. It must integrate seamlessly with
existing sputtering facility installed at the University of Hyderabad. The architecture shall be modular and
pgradable, enabling future enhancements and addition of advanced features.
