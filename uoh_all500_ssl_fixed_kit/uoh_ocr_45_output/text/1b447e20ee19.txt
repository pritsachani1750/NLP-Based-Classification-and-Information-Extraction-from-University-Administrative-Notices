The admissions for classes UKG, II to V and classes VII to IX for the academic
year 2025-2026 are open.

Applications are invited for admission into classes UKG, JI to V and classes VII to IX.
Application forms can be obtained from the office of the UoH Campus school from 09.06.2025
on payment of Rs.400/- on all working days from 11.00 am to 2 p.m. Candidates belonging to
SC/ST/PWD have to pay only Rs.200/- for application form. Application fee has to be paid by
way of challan in SBI, HCU Branch.

Duly filled in application form, along with the following documents has to be submitted on or
before 16.06.2025 at the UoH Campus School office on all working days from 11A.M. to 2P.M.
1. Birth certificate.
2. Community/Caste certificate in case of SC/ST/OBC candidates.
3. Progress report for the academic year 2024-25.
