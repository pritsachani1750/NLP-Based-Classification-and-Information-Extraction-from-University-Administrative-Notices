(3) Land issues:

After careful consideration of the Minutes of the Meetings and
recommendations of the Prof. Sanjay Subodh Committee on land matters,
the Council resolved to initiate measures to secure the land in possession
of the university by approaching the State Government for the transfer of
title deeds to the land and constructing a boundary wall around it after the
mutation of the title deeds.

The Council also noted with concern the developments related to the parcel
of land in and around the Sy. Nos. 68 and 69 admeasuring Ac. 1.00 gts.,
the efforts made by the University to safeguard its interests, and resolved
to formally register its protest and convey its collective concerns to the
CCLA, the District Collector and other Revenue Authorities, and the State
Government, regarding the survey conducted behind its back on the said
parcel of land and the unilateral declaration of the Revenue Department
that the said land admeasuring Ac. 1.00 gts. in the Sy. Nos. 68 and 69
belongs to “M/s Saahi System Private Limited.”

(4) Appeals of the complainant and respondent in ICC Case (F.No.207):

complainant in the ICC Case (File No.207) and the appeal dated 13-08-2025

Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), the Council resolved that there is no

(5) Representation of the respondent in ICC case (F.No.217):

A PhD scholar filed a complaint dated 24 May, 2025, against a faculty
member in the same department, with the Internal Complaints Committee
(ICC) and the Committee, after holding an enquiry into the complaint on its
File No. 217, recommended the following action against said faculty vide its
report dated 10.09.2025:

1. To withhold his next promotion for three years, along with withholding
annual increments during the penalty period,

2. To relieve him immediately from any administrative bodies and positions
held by him presently in the university, and not to entrust any further
administrative positions for a period of three years,

3. He shall submit an assurance in writing undertaking that he would not
trouble or hinder the progress of the complainant both academically and
professionally, nor make any public statements that would affect her
career at any time.

4. He shall not supervise any academic activity of the complainant.

The Council, after carefully considering the representation dated
30/10/2025 from the faculty concerned (Respondent) against the above
ICC recommendations, and the ICC report and recommendations, resolved
to confirm the inquiry conducted by the ICC and decided to impose the
following penalties:

1. To withhold one annual increment due for a period of one year without
cumulative effect, during which his further promotions shall also be
withheld,

2. To relieve him immediately from any administrative positions held by him
presently in the university, and not to entrust any further administrative
positions for a period of three years,

3. He shall submit an assurance in writing undertaking that he would not
trouble or hinder the progress of the complainant both academically and
professionally, nor make any public statements that would affect her
career at any time.

4. He shall not supervise any academic activity of the complainant.
