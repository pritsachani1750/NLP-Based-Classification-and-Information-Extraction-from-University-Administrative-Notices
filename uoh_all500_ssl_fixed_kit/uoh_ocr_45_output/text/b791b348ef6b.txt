The Committee then took up the regular Agenda of the Meeting for discussion.

FC:89:2025:1

To confirm the Minutes of the 88 meeting of the Finance Committee held on
25 June, 2025

As no comments were received, the Minutes of the 88h meeting of the Finance
Committee held on 25th June, 2025 were confirmed. The Committee advised the
University to comply with the relevant statutory provisions of University / Govt. of India /
CVC strictly.

FC:89:2025:2

To report the action taken on the Minutes of the 88th Meeting of the Finance
Committee held on 25t June, 2025

The Committee noted the action taken by the University (wherever required) on the
Minutes of the 88" meeting of the Finance Committee held on 25 June, 2025.

FC:89:2025:3

To consider, approve and authorize the Vice-Chancellor to forward Audited
Annual Accounts of the University for the FY 2024-25 (along with the final
Separate Audit Report and reply of the University thereto) to the Ministry of
Education, New Delhi, after obtaining the approval of the Executive Council
and Court, for causing them to be laid on the tables of both the Houses of
Parliament

The Committee was appraised that draft Separate Audit Report was received on
25.10.2025 and the University has furnished its reply on 29.10.2025. Receipt of final
Separate Audit Report is awaited. The Committee considered & approved the Audited
Annual Accounts of the University for the FY 2024-25, the draft Separate Audit Report
(SAR) and the reply of the University thereto. The Committee, while advising the
University to comply with the observations contained in the draft / final SAR has
authorized the Vice-Chancellor to forward the same to Ministry of Education, New Delhi
for them to be laid on the tables of both the Houses of Parliament after obtaining the
approval of the Executive Council and the Court.

FC:89:2025:4

To report the status of grants received in respect of Salaries & Recurring,
Capital Assets for the FY 2025-26 vis-a-vis expenditure incurred thereof as
on 30.09.2025

The Committee noted the status of grants received by the University in respect of
Salaries, Recurring & Capital Assets for the FY 2025-26 vis-a-vis expenditure incurred
thereof as on 30.9.2025. The Committee was appraised of the need to enhance the non-
salary budget, owing to increase in the maintenance costs of the old buildings,
construction of new academic buildings under HEFA/EWS, etc. The Committee
recommended to UGC/MoE to consider the projections of UoH in toto at RE stage.

FC:89:2025:5

To consider the proposed Revised Estimates 2025-26 and Budget Estimates
2026-27 for Salaries, Recurring & Creation of Assets

The Finance Officer explained the salient features of the RE 2025-26 and BE 2026-27
and informed the Committee that the estimates are based on a realistic assessment of
requirement of funds for the University.

FC:89:2025:8 | To consider and approve the Minutes of the meeting of the 61st Building
Committee held on 29th October, 2025

After going through the Minutes of the 61st Building Committee held on 29.10.2025, the
Committee approved the same.

FC:89:2025:9 | Physical and Financial progress of the ongoing construction activities

The University Engineer made a Power Point Presentation on the progress of all the
construction works. The Committee noted the same and advised the University to

e ensure that Works / Building Committee of UoH monitor and approve all the
ongoing construction works periodically to avoid cost and time overruns.

e ensure that abstract cost and rate conformity certificate and other codal
formalities for each building project are as per the relevant UGC guidelines /
relevant provisions of GFR 2017 and CVC.

e ensure that all major infrastructure projects are taken up only through HEFA and
not through MoE/UGC grants under OH-35.

e ensure that all the minor original / repair works taken up for the Campus
Development are done with the prior approval of the Building Committee /
Finance Committee.

e ensure that no part of the internal receipts or the interest earned on these receipts
are spent on these works.

FC:89:2025:10 | Status of Pending Audit Paras

Noted. The Committee advised the University to ensure that the remaining pending Audit
Paras are dropped at the earliest by providing required information / clarification to the
AG Audit. It was assured to the Committee that the University will take all required steps
to settle the remaining paras also as soon as possible.

The meeting ended with a vote of thanks to the Chair.
