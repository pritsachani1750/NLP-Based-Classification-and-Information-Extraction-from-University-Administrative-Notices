ANNEXURE-I

TERMS AND CONDITIONS FOR RATE CONTRACT
FOR SUPPLY OF ALLOPATHIC MEDICINES

This tender enquiry is for the purpose of executing Rate contract for supply of allopathic lifesaving
medicines for the treatment of hypertension, coronary artery disease, diabetes, Asthma,

cerebrovascular accident, epilepsy, psychosis, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder & depression which are
used in Health Centre, University of Hyderabad, Hyderabad.

The vendor must have access to ensure proper storage and to maintain require temperature during
transport of medicines until it reaches to Health Centre of University of Hyderabad

The successful bidder shall supply and distribute the Branded Medicines, on daily basis to the
beneficiaries listed in the Health Card issued by the University of Hyderabad as per the indents issued
by the Doctors of the Health Centre of University of Hyderabad once in a month only to the eligible
employees and dependents. The average number of prescriptions per day may range between 30 and
40 and types of medicines to be delivered per day would be in range of 350-400. Indents are based on
prescriptions from Health Centre Doctors / Doctors from University recognized hospitals.

The value of medicines to be supplied would approximately be in the range of Rs.9.5 Lakh to Rs.10
Lakh per month.

The vendor shall commence supply and distribution of the branded “admissible lifesaving medicines”
which are generally prescribed for chronic ailments from the day awarding the contract and the vendor
should ensure that sufficient stock of such medicines is kept in the Pharmacy.

The vendor should complete the supply and delivery of medicines on the next working day (within 20
hours i.e. collection of prescription by 4.00 PM, supply of medicines next morning (next working day)
latest by 12.00 Noon) of issue of prescriptions and at one go only (not in piece meal). Failure to deliver
the medicines within the stipulated time would entail levy of penalty. Substitute medicines should not
be supplied without the written approval of Chief Medical Officer (CMO)/Medical Officer (MO).

The vendor shall arrange the following man-power for collection of prescription from Health Centre and
supply of medicines to the patients / attendants of the patient:

a) 1 (one) qualified Pharmacist having thorough knowledge of medicines

b) 2 (two) helpers/Attendants for supply and distribution of medicines.

The pharmacist and supporting staff of vendor should be present from 10.00 a.m to 04.00 p.m at
University Health Centre on all working days.

The location of Pharmacy is at University of Hyderabad Health Centre. The vendor will be provided
adequate space free of charge within the premises of Health Centre with lock and key facility to store
the medicines. The vendor should have his own Refrigerator at Pharmacy. The Vendor should arrange
Personal Computer with printer and appropriate software so that the data, such as patient ID, patient
name and medicines supplied can be entered into and accessed whenever required. The data should
be shared online with the office of CMO and office of Finance officer. The University will provide
necessary logistics support in installing this facility and will also provide LAN internet facility. The cost
of establishment including salaries for this purpose shall be borne by the Vendor.

At the time of delivery of medicines, the vendor should generate invoice (in triplicate) against each

indent. The vendor shall obtain acknowledgement of the patient / attendant on the reverse side of the
invoice and the indent.

The vendor should submit the invoices along with a computer-generated consolidated statement (in
triplicate) to the Chief Medical Officer & in-charge Health Centre once in every 15 days for processing
of payment. The bill so submitted will be settled within one month in consultation with finance
thereafter. The fortnightly bill should contain the full details with regard to the date, ID No. and name
of the employee, name of medicines, description and quantity of medicines supplied, Manufacturing
Date, Batch No and Expiry date, MRP, discount and net price of each of the medicine and the total
